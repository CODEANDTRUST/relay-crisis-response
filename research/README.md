# Relay — Research & Design

This folder is the evidence base for Relay. It is a worked example of evidence-led product design: the work moves from raw user voices, through synthesis, to a defensible design document and a brand system. The guiding rule throughout was that every design implication must trace back to a verbatim quote from a real user.

## Suggested reading order

1. **[`user-research-summary.md`](user-research-summary.md)** — Start here. A 10-minute distillation of what crisis-response practitioners actually need, drawn from 1,780 Reddit posts and 108 YouTube transcripts.
2. **[`competitive-research-summary.md`](competitive-research-summary.md)** — What the existing tools do and where they fail, synthesized from 250+ App Store, Google Play, and Reddit reviews across five competitors.
3. **[`design-plan.md`](design-plan.md)** — The design document the research produced: product thesis, primary user goals, non-goals, user flows, screen inventory, information architecture, functionality prioritization, technical integration notes, and a competitor differentiation table.
4. **[`STYLES.md`](STYLES.md)** — The Relay brand and style guide: color, typography, components, iconography, and motion.

## A note on sources

All research material is drawn from publicly posted content — app-store reviews, Google Play reviews, public Reddit threads, and public YouTube transcripts. `user-research-long.md` is the raw, lightly processed corpus; quoted passages remain the work of their original authors and are included here for research and design-reference purposes. Competitor names and product details reflect what was publicly available at the time the research was run (May 2026).
